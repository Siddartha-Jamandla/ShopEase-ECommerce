Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShopEase handles information within this demonstration e-commerce website.

1. Information We Collect

When using this demo, information such as your name, email address, phone number and delivery details may be entered into forms.

2. Local Storage

This project uses browser localStorage to demonstrate features such as login information, wishlist items, shopping cart data and orders. This information is stored locally in your browser.

3. Payments

This front-end project does not process real payments. Payment methods displayed during checkout are for demonstration purposes only.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The demo website does not intentionally implement advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may load fonts and icons from external services such as Google Fonts and Font Awesome.

6. Data Security

Because this is a front-end demonstration, localStorage should not be considered a secure database for real customer information.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the ShopEase project develops.
